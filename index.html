GA4 website analytics & reporting

Analytics practice

GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio

Details▾
What I set up

A GA4 account and property from scratch, a web data stream, and a manually installed Google tag — then verified everything through Real-Time reporting before trusting the standard reports.

What I read

Traffic acquisition by channel, engagement rate against the Shopping & Retailers peer benchmark, new vs. returning users, geography, browser/device/OS splits and a Lifetime Value report (flat, since no revenue events were configured yet).

Key finding

Referral traffic was only 11% of users but converted to a 100% engagement rate, versus 44% for Direct — a pattern worth acting on once real traffic starts.

Recommendation I made

Configure GA4 Key Events (add-to-cart, checkout) since none were firing yet, and migrate the manual tag to GTM before adding more tracking.

Performance marketing knowledge

Metrics I track

CTR
Click-through rate

Clicks ÷ impressions. Rises with more relevant ads or better audience targeting; falls with ad fatigue or a mismatched audience.

CPC
Cost per click

What you pay per click on average. Rises with more competition for keywords or a lower Quality/Relevance score.

CPM
Cost per 1,000 impressions

What you pay to reach 1,000 people. Rises during high-demand periods like festive season.

CPA
Cost per acquisition

Spend ÷ conversions. Falls as targeting and landing pages improve; rises with weak conversion tracking or budget spent on the wrong audience.

CPL
Cost per lead

Spend ÷ leads generated. Watched closely on lead-gen campaigns to judge whether the funnel is efficient.

CVR
Conversion rate

Conversions ÷ clicks or sessions. A strong signal of landing page and offer quality, separate from ad quality.

ROAS
Return on ad spend

Revenue ÷ ad spend. Needs accurate GA4/Ads conversion tracking with values attached to be meaningful.

Impr.
Impressions

How many times an ad was shown. Grows with budget and reach; can be inflated by very broad targeting.

Reach
Unique reach

Distinct people who saw the ad, as opposed to total impressions.

Freq.
Frequency

Average times one person saw the ad. Too high, and creative fatigue sets in — CTR drops.

Eng. rate
Engagement rate

In GA4: engaged sessions ÷ total sessions. An engaged session lasts 10+ seconds, has a key event, or 2+ page views.
